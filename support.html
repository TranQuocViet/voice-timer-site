Frequently asked questions

I can't hear the voice.

Turn up the media volume with the volume buttons while the timer is running, and check that no Bluetooth device is connected that you aren't using. You can use Settings → Test voice in the app to check the voice at any time.

Can I change the voice language?

Yes. Voice Timer speaks English, Vietnamese, French, and Spanish. Choose a language in the app's Settings — it applies to both the interface and the voice.

How do I remove ads?

Open the app's Settings and tap the purchase button in the Remove ads section. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

I already paid to remove ads, but I still see them.

Open the app's Settings and tap Restore purchase. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it. Purchases carry over to any iPhone signed in with that account.

How does iCloud sync work?

iCloud sync is included with Remove ads. It keeps your workout history in step across iPhones signed in to the same iCloud account. You can turn it on or off in the iCloud sync section of the app's Settings. If you're not signed in to iCloud, your history is simply kept on this device.

How do I delete my workout history?

Go to the History screen and tap Delete all. If iCloud sync is on, this also deletes the history on your other devices, and it can't be undone.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

If you're in a region where consent is required, open the app's Settings and tap Privacy options. To change tracking permission, go to iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Does the app use my microphone?

No. "Voice" means the app speaks the time aloud. It never listens or records. See our Privacy Policy for details.
