Privacy Policy — Voice Timer

Last updated: October 3, 2026

This policy applies to the Voice Timer app for iPhone (a workout timer that speaks the time aloud), published on the App Store. We want to explain clearly and honestly what data the app does — and does not — handle.

1. Summary

  • Voice Timer requires no account and runs no server of its own that collects user data.
  • The app does not record audio and does not access the microphone. "Voice" means the app speaks the time aloud using your device's built-in text-to-speech — it does not listen to voice commands.
  • Your settings (timer mode, speech language, speech rate, etc.) are stored locally on your device and are never sent to us.
  • If you have bought the ad-removal purchase, your workout history syncs across your Apple devices through your own iCloud account. Apple stores this data; we cannot access it. You can turn syncing off in the app's Settings.
  • The app shows ads served by Google AdMob and offers an in-app purchase to remove them. These are the only cases where a third party processes data, as detailed below.

2. Data we do not collect

Voice Timer does not collect, store, or share your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files, audio recordings, or any content you create. The app has no sign-in, no user profiles, and uses no analytics tools of its own.

3. Data stored on your device

The app saves your preferences (for example: timer duration, countdown or count-up mode, speech language and rate, announcement interval, and whether the ad-removal purchase is active) in local storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

The app also keeps your workout history (start time, duration, timer mode) on your device. If you have bought the ad-removal purchase and are signed in to iCloud, this history is also stored in the iCloud Key-Value Store of your Apple Account so it can sync across your devices. It stays in your private iCloud and never passes through any server of ours. Turning iCloud sync off in the app's Settings stops sending history to iCloud.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version displays ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may automatically collect certain data from your device, such as:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) — only if you allow tracking;
  • IP address, general device information, and operating system version;
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, taps) and diagnostics.

This collection is performed by Google under Google's own policies. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your consent: Where the law requires it (for example in the EU/EEA, the UK, and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before serving ads. You can decline personalized ads and still use the app normally, and you can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy options.

Tracking permission: The app may show a short explanation, followed by Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", ads still appear but are not personalized based on your activity in other apps and websites, and no app feature is affected. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

5. In-app purchase (remove ads)

The app offers a one-time purchase that removes ads. The entire transaction is handled by the Apple App Store. We never receive or store your card details, payment account, or identity — the app only receives a "purchased / not purchased" signal from Apple to turn ads off. Payment processing is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Device features the app uses

  • Keep screen awake: prevents the display from turning off while the timer runs. No personal data involved.
  • Text-to-speech: the app passes the numbers and phrases to be spoken to the on-device speech engine. The app does not use the microphone.
  • Internet: used to load ads, verify purchases (sections 4 and 5), and sync workout history through iCloud (section 3).

7. Children

Voice Timer is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us and we will help.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Since Voice Timer stores no personal data on any server of ours, the most direct way to delete all local data is to uninstall the app. Workout history on this device is always deleted when you use "Delete all" in the app's History screen; if you have bought the ad-removal purchase, turned sync on, and are signed in to iCloud, this also deletes the synced history on every other device using the same iCloud account — if any of those three conditions is missing, it only affects this device. For data processed by Google for advertising, you can reset or limit your advertising identifier in iOS settings, visit Google My Ad Center, and change your consent choice inside the app as described in section 4.

9. Security

The app keeps settings and workout history in your device's local storage (workout history also syncs through your private iCloud if you bought the ad-removal purchase and keep sync on), and transmits no personal data to servers of ours. Connections to Google and Apple services use those platforms' standard secure protocols.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes (for example when the app gains new features), we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date at the top. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes on the App Store.
